## About the E-Book

*This publication is in Portuguese.*

The immense volume of data held by governments can only become an invaluable asset when properly collected, processed, and unified under quality and governance principles.

This e-book, published by LabLivre, offers a **practical guide** for public managers to orchestrate data pipelines with Free Software tools.

### What will you find?
* **Quality Standards** for assessing and integrating open datasets.
* **Data Governance** from the perspective of privacy and accountability.
* Experiences and use cases in **Federal Budget Execution**.
