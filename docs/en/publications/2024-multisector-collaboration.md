## The Brasil Participativo Experience

*This publication is in Portuguese.*

In today's Digital Government landscape, building scalable tools that meet people's demands requires more than just code – it requires mature multisector arrangements involving academia, government, and civil society.

In this paper, we reflect on the historical process, the sociotechnical challenges, and the outcomes of building **Brasil Participativo**. We highlight how a Free Software initiative such as *Decidim* could be collaboratively adapted to reach the largest scale of popular participation in the history of Brazil's Executive Branch, in record time.
