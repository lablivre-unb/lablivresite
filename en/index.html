Research Lines

DevOps

Study and application of practices and team structures that integrate development and operations to improve continuous delivery, software quality, and reliability.

MLOps

Research on processes, tools, and automation for developing, deploying, monitoring, and governing machine learning models in production.

Data Engineering

Investigation of data architectures, pipelines, and governance for large-scale collection, integration, quality, and analysis.

Experience-Based Learning

Teaching and training approaches based on practice, experimentation, and solving real problems in academic and professional contexts.

Co-development Methodology

Collaborative software development methods involving researchers, students, users, and institutional partners.

Free Software Ecosystems

Study of the technical, organizational, and social dynamics of free software communities and platforms, focusing on sustainability and impact.

Digital Participation

Research on technologies and methods to broaden, improve, and analyze citizen participation in digital decision-making processes.

Publications

September 2025

A practical guide to integrating and qualifying public data

An e-book with our methodology and practical guide for turning scattered data into intelligent decisions. Published in Portuguese.

January 2019

Public Money, Public Code

Brazilian Portuguese translation of the publication "Public Money Public Code – Modernising Public Infrastructure with Free Software" by the Free Software Foundation Europe.

November 2019

A survey of DevOps concepts and challenges

This survey investigates and discusses the challenges of DevOps from the perspective of engineers, managers, and researchers. A paper with more than 700 citations.

July 2025

An Integration Platform for the Structuring Systems of Federal Financial Execution

This paper presents an experience report on implementing an open source platform to integrate data from different Structuring Systems of federal budget execution (SIOP, SIAFI, SIAPE, TransfereGov, and ContratoGov). Published in Portuguese.

August 2024

Multisector collaboration for the development and maintenance of participation technologies

This paper reflects on multisector arrangements for developing and maintaining digital tools, based on the creation process of the Brasil Participativo digital platform. Published in Portuguese.

November 2023

Harmonizing DevOps taxonomies—A grounded theory study

This paper presents empirical research on the structure of DevOps teams in software-producing organizations. The goal is to deepen the understanding of how these teams are organized and what characterizes them.
